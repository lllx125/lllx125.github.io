LightSaber Fight

A VR combat game for Meta Quest 3. Strike drones with a lightsaber, turn their bullets back on them, and use the Force to pull enemies closer or throw rocks into their path.

Jun 2024 Meta Quest 3
Code Gameplay demo Quest 3 build

Overview

I built LightSaber Fight in Unity as a VR game for Meta Quest 3. It combines close-range saber combat with projectile defense and remote object interaction: grab a rock, throw it at a drone, or pull the drone into reach.

The game uses C# gameplay scripts and Unity’s XR Interaction Toolkit. Its tutorial introduces grabbing and saber activation before the player enters a dark arena with approaching drones, incoming fire, a health display, and a score counter.

Game systems

A responsive saber

The blade extends and retracts on activation, with lighting and an ignition sound. Swing audio responds to movement speed, while collisions trigger hit sounds and different responses for drones, rocks, and bullets.

Deflected bullets reverse velocity and receive a small directional variation. The score system distinguishes saber attacks, rock impacts, and returned projectiles.

Enemies and physical objects

Drones spawn at randomized positions and intervals, turn toward the player’s area, and fire on a timer. They can be defeated through repeated saber hits or collisions with rocks and projectiles.

Grabbing a rock switches it from scripted movement to physics-driven interaction. Impacts shatter its mesh with an accompanying sound. Incoming rocks and bullets reduce player health; reaching zero ends the run.
