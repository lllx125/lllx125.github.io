Overview

One Two Dash is a 2D platformer built by our BAL team: Bobby Zhang, Andy Yang, and me. The knight moves through a scrolling world of goblins, crystals, and obstacles, with encounters against a fire-breathing dragon.

I led the three-person team during a 24-hour game jam and worked on the core mechanics, level design, and art in Unity. The team created the game’s hand-drawn imagery and original music and sound effects.

How to play

Space ×3DashStrike goblins along your path
CrystalsCollect pointsBreak them on contact
GoblinsDash to defeatContact without a dash ends the run
ObstaclesAvoid in every stateDashing does not pass through them

Game systems

One input, three-beat rhythm

A three-state input counter makes the first two presses jump and the third dash. Jumping switches the knight between the ceiling and floor; dashing moves the player forward and changes the scrolling speed, with rotation and audio marking the attack.

A changing path and a dragon

A timed generator places obstacles, crystals, and goblins on either path at randomized intervals. Boss encounters transition into a separate scene, where the dragon alternates its fire between the upper and lower routes.

A persistent game manager carries the score across scenes. The game awards points for crystals, defeated goblins, and a completed boss encounter.
